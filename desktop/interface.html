How the measurements work

Mean intensity is calculated from original pixels in each cell mask. Background-corrected mean = cell mean − background median for that field and channel. Background excludes the segmented objects, bright pixels in the chosen reference channel, and a surrounding margin. Check the background overlay in the results. Dense fields or unusual staining may need different settings.

The result page also shows an inner edge and an interior region. These are geometric regions, not identified plasma membranes, nuclei, or Golgi. Negative corrected values are retained. Unequal exposure/gain and saturated images require interpretation before comparing samples.
