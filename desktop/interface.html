How values are calculated

The mean intensity of a cell is calculated from the original pixels inside its outline. Background-corrected mean = cell mean − the background estimate for that image and channel. Both the background mean and median are recorded, so the estimate can be changed in Review without analysing again.

The background is every pixel that is not in a cell found in the chosen channel, not brighter than the cut-off in the chosen fluorescence channel, and not within the margin around either. Check the background region in Review; dense fields or unusual staining may need different settings.

Review also shows an inner boundary band and an interior region. These are geometric regions, not identified plasma membranes, nuclei or Golgi. Negative corrected values are kept. Unequal exposure or gain and saturated images need interpretation before samples are compared.

Each channel chosen under Find cells is segmented on its own and gives an independent set of objects; channels are not combined. Cellpose enlarges each image by 30 / cell diameter before segmentation, so time and memory grow quickly for small diameters; a run is refused when the enlarged image would exceed 8192 × 8192 pixels. A GPU failure is reported, not silently rerun: choose CPU under More settings and start again.
