Methods and interpretation

Scope

Cellpose outputs candidate regions; a complete run does not establish cell identity or segmentation accuracy. No nuclear/perinuclear analysis, image registration, exposure normalization or biological statistical tests are performed.

Background: anchor-mask-mad-v1

Use “Show background” to inspect the exact sampled region. A sufficient number of pixels does not prove that region is free of fluorescence. This method is independent of the historical pilot background values.

Measurements

Corrected mean = original ROI mean − field/channel background median. Corrected integrated intensity = raw sum − area × background median. Negative values are retained. Empty regions and invalid background corrections are missing. Saturated counts use the recorded limit, when known. Saturation flags indicate censored intensities.

Regions and display

Auto display uses field/channel p10–p99.9 with an asinh transform. Fixed display uses a shared linear range within each channel across this run. Display contrast and resized previews never enter measurement. Equal display scales do not correct unequal exposure or gain.

Acquisition metadata

Filter names, wavelengths and display colors do not establish fluorophore, construct or organelle identity. Biological labels require researcher confirmation.

Export

Optional ratios are exploratory display-only values; the CSV exports their underlying intensities. The included CSV uses the export segmentation scope above the viewer and excludes every row of manually excluded candidates. Display and measurement selectors do not restrict exported measurement channels or regions. The original CSV retains all measurements. QC decisions are local to this browser and can be saved or restored as JSON; unmarked candidates have not necessarily been reviewed. Source and model checksums, configuration, versions, masks and validation results are saved beside this viewer. This page performs no new inference and does not upload images.
